PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE
	EN	Podcast Cache

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_DESC
	EN	Download podcast episodes to disk and play them locally (needs the built-in Podcasts plugin)

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_STATUS
	EN	Status

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_STATUS_DESC
	EN	Whether cached episodes are being played from disk, and anything stopping that. Reload the page to refresh.

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_CACHED
	EN	Cached episodes

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_CACHED_DESC
	EN	What is in the cache folder, one folder per podcast.

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_HOSTS
	EN	Podcast servers

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_HOSTS_DESC
	EN	How politely each server is being treated. Background downloads from one server are spaced out by a gap that adjusts itself: it shrinks while downloads go smoothly, and grows when a server pushes back (asks us to slow down, refuses, or gets much slower than usual). A server that asks us to wait is left alone until it's ready. Playing an episode is never held up by the gap.

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_EVENTS
	EN	Recent activity

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_EVENTS_DESC
	EN	The last 100 things this plugin did, newest first. Cleared when LMS restarts.

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_CACHE_ROOT
	EN	Cache folder

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_CACHE_ROOT_DESC
	EN	Where downloaded episodes are kept. To keep episodes out of your music library, use a folder whose name starts with a dot (the scanner skips those), or one outside your music folders.

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_PLAY_WAIT
	EN	Wait for the download

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_PLAY_WAIT_DESC
	EN	When you play an episode that isn't cached yet, it is downloaded first and then played from disk. If the download takes longer than this, the episode streams as before while the download finishes for next time. 0 streams straight away.

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_PREFETCH
	EN	Automatic downloads

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_PREFETCH_DESC
	EN	Download the newest episodes of each podcast ahead of time, so they play from disk the first time. It downloads as many as the podcast keeps (below); for "all episodes", the newest 3; for "only the episode playing", none. Episodes are also checked whenever you browse a podcast. Quiet hours stop new background downloads starting, e.g. during a nightly backup; playing an episode still downloads it.

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_DEFAULT_KEEP
	EN	Episodes to keep

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_DEFAULT_KEEP_DESC
	EN	How many episodes of each podcast to keep on disk, unless set below. Older episodes are deleted when a new one arrives, when a feed is read, or when you stop playing, but never one in a player's playlist, or one you're part-way through.

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_FEEDS
	EN	Per podcast

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_FEEDS_DESC
	EN	Override the number of episodes kept for individual podcasts. These are your subscriptions from the built-in Podcasts plugin.

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_LABEL_CACHED
	EN	cached

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_LABEL_DOWNLOADING
	EN	downloading

PLUGIN_PODCASTCACHE_LABEL_QUEUED
	EN	queued
